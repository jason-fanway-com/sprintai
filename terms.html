2. What OrderFare does

Payments are made to the shop's own Stripe account.

The confirmed order is the record. Before paying, the customer receives a read-back of the order and its total and confirms it. That confirmed order is the record of what was ordered and what it cost, and it is what the shop receives on its kitchen ticket.

Who delivers is set when the shop is set up. By default a shop's delivery orders are delivered by Uber. A shop that delivers with its own staff chooses that at set-up; only OrderFare can change it afterwards. Each order is delivered the way it was quoted, even if the setting later changes.

The customer's delivery fee and driver tip are paid to OrderFare at checkout, and OrderFare pays Uber. Couriers leave orders at the door unless the customer asks otherwise. 5. Payments, fees, cancellations and refunds

How a payment is split

Each customer payment is made to the shop's Stripe account. From it, OrderFare receives its per-order fee (currently $1.49, paid by the customer, not the shop) and, on Uber deliveries, the delivery fee and tip it pays to Uber. The shop receives the food and sales tax. Stripe charges its card fee on each payment: the shop pays Stripe's fixed per-transaction fee and the percentage on its food and tax; OrderFare pays the percentage on its own share, which it deducts from what it receives.

Monthly fee

OrderFare's monthly fee is $99 per shop, billed by OrderFare. Design partners receive their first six months free. Pricing changes are communicated in advance.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Only the shop cancels or refunds a paid order, and only through OrderFare (the Cancel / refund control or the shop chat). OrderFare never decides whether a customer gets a refund. A customer who asks OrderFare to cancel is directed to the shop.
  • The courier is cancelled first. If Uber is delivering and has not picked up the food, OrderFare cancels the courier before any refund. Uber charges nothing if no driver has accepted, and a cancellation fee (currently $5.00) once a driver has accepted.
  • If the shop can't fill the order, the customer is refunded in full. Any Uber cancellation fee caused by the shop's cancellation is charged to the shop and recovered from its next orders.
  • If the customer cancels, the shop decides how much of the food to refund (none, part, or all). OrderFare refunds its fee, the delivery fee and the tip, less any Uber cancellation fee, which the customer bears. Once the driver has the food, the delivery fee and tip are owed to the driver and are not refunded, and OrderFare's fee is kept.
  • Wrong or missing items, or a customer who is unhappy with the food, are the shop's to resolve. Any refund comes from the shop's part of the payment.
  • If no one is there to receive a delivery, the courier leaves it at the door. If Uber returns an order the customer could not receive, no refund is due.
  • Card fees are not returned on refunds. Stripe keeps its fee when a payment is refunded; that cost stays with whoever's share is refunded, except where the refund is caused by an OrderFare error (section 3).
  • Refunds made outside OrderFare, for example directly in Stripe, come entirely from the shop's account and are not adjusted for delivery money OrderFare received or paid; OrderFare will help square them up if asked.
