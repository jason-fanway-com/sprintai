## 3. The defect classes found today

1. **Category terms blocked derived item terms.** A category named "Pizza" suppressed the
   item term `pizza` for every pizza, and `resolveItem` cannot read category rows. Fixed
   `a258f801`. Vito's was green only because its categories never collided with its head
   nouns.
2. **Choice terms did the same.** 44 suppressed head nouns at Zio's, 16 at NJB, **39 at
   Vito's** — `bagel` at a bagel shop and `rye` at Vito's were both dead ends. Fixed
   `fb412089`. Safe because ANSWER has structural priority over the lexicon: PROPOSE sits
   in the `else` branch of `if (answerResult.resolved)`, so an item term cannot hijack an
   open question.
3. **Slot-key collisions.** NJB had 12 items whose two required slots both carried
   `slot_key: "choice"`, rendering identical questions — the customer could not tell them
   apart. Fixed in two steps: use the parser's captured `label` (`5639013c`, closed 1 of
   12), then fall back to its captured `anchor` (`4fe70435`, closed the remaining 11).
   0 of 12 survive.
4. **Unanchored ordinal matching.** `matchOrdinalPosition` matched a number anywhere in a
   message, so `"10 pieces"` picked candidate #10 and `"two cheeseburgers and a large
   fries"` picked candidate #2. Live on the deployed build. Fixed `2d725b2d`.
5. **Negation never checked on the slot path.** `isNegated` existed and was wired only to
   the modifier branch. Fixed `61c2eeea`.
6. **Numbered choices indistinguishable after stemming.** `significantStems` drops tokens
   under 3 characters, so `10 Pieces` and `20 Pieces` both reduced to `{"piec"}`. Made
   numerics significant **only within a colliding group** (`83f18a27`) — 2 groups out of
   186, both Zio's wings.

## 4. The wings money bug — CLOSED, `0d259996`. Kept for the reasoning.

A bare `"2"` answering Boneless Wings' piece-count silently selects `"20 Pieces"`
(+$8.00). Confirmed live: the model asserted it on **5 of 6 identical calls**.

The mechanism is known. The deterministic matcher correctly refuses to guess, the turn
falls through to PROPOSE, and `decide()`'s structured ADD path (`turn-engine.ts:609`)
trusts the model's assertion unconditionally — `resolveChoiceDisplays`
(`turn-engine.ts:493`) checks only that the `choice_id` names a real choice, never that it
is the one the customer meant. `decide()` receives no customer message to check against.

**This is the item-identity problem at the choice level.** `resolve-item.ts` exists because
item identity cannot be a model output; choice identity still is.

Scope: 2 groups, both Zio's wings (10|20 and 8|14 Pieces). Exhaustive scan of 715 items —
0 at Vito's, 0 at NJB.

**Three attempts to scope a fix were wrong, all caught empirically by the crew:**
- gating the shared `resolveAskPlan` broke 4 pre-existing tests — it also blocks every
  one-shot add like "medium cheeseburger", because the structured path passes
  `customerMessage = ""`
- narrowing to `answer()`'s slot call closed nothing — that call site already passes `[]`
  for `modelAssertedChoiceTexts` and was never on the bug's path
- validating `add.choices` against `add.item_span` is dead — the span is the item name
  only, and varies run-to-run on identical input

**Dead end, do not retry:** making numeric tokens globally significant. 8 tests change; the
3-char floor is deliberate and a test comment cites Jason's own `large`/`medium` repro.

**Closed by `0d259996` on the fourth attempt**, without touching `decide()`. The unlock:
`83f18a27` already computes the set of groups whose choices are indistinguishable to the
deterministic matcher — exactly 2 of 186. Those are precisely the groups where the model's
assertion cannot be checked, so those are the only groups that require textual support.
Everywhere else is untouched, which is what the three earlier attempts got wrong.
Proven live on Zio's: a bare `"2"` now re-asks and no $8.00 appears.

## 4c. OPEN AND UNEXPLAINED — the name / order-type retries

Live on all three shops: `"pickup"` does not advance, `"pick up"` does; `"Joe"` does not
advance, `"Joe Smith"` does. Stored values (`00-BS`) are `"Joe Smith"` / `"pickup"`, so the
second attempt is what landed and the first genuinely failed. But run by hand against the
real source, `/\bpick[\s-]?up\b/i.test("pickup")` is **true** and
`looksLikeCustomerName("Joe")` is **true**.

**Four PO hypotheses were wrong. Do not reason from source for the fifth.** Dead ends:
the 3-word cap (bare "Joe" passes it); the compound-word regex theory (it matches);
`isPickupNameExtraction`/`isExplicitNameExtraction` (**these functions do not exist** —
`00-BP` was a confabulated report and was rejected); and a stale `shopContext` causing a
one-turn lag (`00-BS` disproves it).

**Start by instrumenting `answer()` on a live turn.** Every wrong hypothesis today came
from reading source instead of observing the running system — that is the method that was
unavailable to the PO all day and it is the one that would have saved four rounds.

Impact: friction on two steps every customer passes. No wrong charge, no cart mutation.

## 5. Also open

- **4 matchers reachable but never falsified:** `isPendingDisambiguationDeclined`,
  `extractPriceCentsFromMessage`, `parseBareTipDollars`, `isRemovalRequested`. The crew
  flagged these as unverified rather than presenting the audit as complete.
- **NJB's recompile vanish count — resolved structurally, and it exposed something else.**
  `_shared/compile-menu.ts` is absent from the `fb412089..4fe70435` diff, so that commit
  cannot have caused the 130; NJB was absorbing compiler generations it had never picked
  up. Verified by the PO. But see the non-idempotency finding below — the vanish number
  itself is not a reliable signal.
- **`service_fee_cents` reads 0** on engine rows before checkout. `persistTurn` writes a
  fee-inclusive `total_cents` but never the fee column. Not a money bug — checkout writes
  it and Stripe never reads it — but any read of a pre-checkout row sees a row that
  contradicts itself.
- **An already-open disambiguation is never re-enqueued** into `pendingAmbiguous`, so an
  answer the resolvers miss abandons the span. Pre-existing.
- **`compile-menu` is not idempotent.** Three consecutive recompiles of unchanged NJB data
  produced three different vanish/move counts. The vanish figure has been used as a
  recompile gate all day; it is not a stable signal.
- **One-shot attribute+item adds have never worked on the engine path.** The model folds
  `"medium cheeseburger"` into `item_span` and returns `choices: []`; `decide()` passes
  `customerMessage = ""`, so nothing can resolve Temp. Verified structurally at `2d725b2d`
  — pre-existing, not caused by today's commits. Two tests preserved in `00-BL` assert a
  contract the model does not produce for this phrasing.
- **`"choice of cheese"` is silently dropped** on NJB's Cheese Omelette Platter — becomes
  neither slot nor modifier, so the option is unrepresented.
- **Coverage gaps, all one cause:** `trailingWordRuns` produces suffixes only, so a medial
  word is never a term. `penne`, `entree`, `dessert`, `large pizza` at Zio's; `bread` at
  NJB. Needs a spec, not a bolt-on — belongs with the onboarding-hardening item in
  `docs/SCALING-BACKLOG.md`.
- **Zio's `pizza` ties 76 candidates.** Safe, but 76 options is not a usable SMS question.
  Product design, not a bug.
- **`"mild"` is non-deterministic** — resolved one slot on two Zio's runs and two slots on
  a third, identical input. Unexplained.

## 7. What would make this phase finishable

Every defect today was found by a human designing a scenario and running it three times.
That does not scale and it is why things keep slipping through. The gate — an automated
harness driving many complete conversations per shop and asserting on DB state, able to go
RED on purpose — is the missing piece, and it converges with Jason's onboarding-hardening
item in `docs/SCALING-BACKLOG.md`.

A reasonable definition of done: all three shops complete an order to a payment link on the
engine path, the multi-turn gate runs green across every shop with a deliberate RED proving
it can fail, and a day passes without a new defect class.
