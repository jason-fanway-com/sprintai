The walkthrough

1. Set it up. "Let me show you your restaurant taking orders by text — the way people already text friends and family. No app to download, nothing to install."

2. Order (QR 1). "Scan this with your camera — it opens a text to your Sprint line. Order something, just like a customer would." Let them order (a bagel, a BOBO). Point out: it understands plain language, confirms the order, totals it, and sends a payment link.

3. 86 an item (QR 2). Have Store Chat already signed in on the owner's phone. "This is your Store Chat, where you run the shop. Tell it you're out of [the item you just ordered]." They 86 it in one message.

4. Prove it (QR 1 again — you). "Watch — I'll order that exact item as a customer." Scan QR 1, order the 86'd item. The assistant says it's out today and suggests an alternative. "That's the thing every owner wishes they had: the second you're out, customers can't order it."

5. The pitch. "You keep your customers, your margin, and your name on the sale. No aggregator taking 30%. It's AI, so it won't be perfect — but we test every shop before go-live, we show you those tests, and we watch quality every single day."

6. Close (QR 3). "Ready to get your own line? Scan this to sign up." Then send them the one-pager below.
