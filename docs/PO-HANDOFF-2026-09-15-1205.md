## 3. What is broken — and how it was missed

**A three-turn conversation fails four ways.** `two cheeseburgers and a large fries` /
`medium` / `thats it`, three runs:

1. "two cheeseburgers" becomes **two separate lines of quantity 1**, not one line of 2.
   Note `2 cheeseburgers, medium` correctly gives ONE line at 2 — the split is path-specific.
2. The temp question is answered for one line and **re-asked forever** for the other.
3. "thats it" while a **slot** question is open still reached PROPOSE and added a **third**
   burger — $25.47. `00-AA` closed this for the order_type question only.
4. The fries are **never mentioned again**. Silently dropped, 3/3.

**Why the gate missed it: my harness sent ONE message per conversation.** A gate that only
tests the first turn will certify a bot that cannot complete an order. That is the single
most important lesson of the day and it was the PO's error, not the crew's.

`~/po-scratch/convogate.py` on the Air is the replacement — it drives each scenario to a
payment link and fails a conversation on duplicate lines, a question asked more than twice, a
subtotal above the expected maximum, or never reaching payment. **It is staged but has never
been run. Smoke-test it on one scenario before trusting a run of it** — two of the PO's own
harnesses had bugs today (a cart_json shape assumption, and the single-turn flaw itself).

## 6. Five lessons this day taught, all now in PO-BRIEF §8

1. **"Built" has three failure shapes** — nothing imports it, a flag is off, or its data is
   silently truncated. All three pass unit tests.
2. **An unbounded PostgREST select returns 1000 rows**, no error. Vito's had 1298.
3. **Isolate with a synthetic fixture** before blaming a module or its data. That separated a
   correct `resolve-item.ts` from a broken loader in two minutes — after a wrong first guess
   that had already been reported to Jason.
4. **A test that reads source code as text is a lint rule, not a test.** Four "inversion"
   enforcement tests were green for weeks while the bot billed the wrong amount.
5. **A single-turn gate certifies a bot that cannot hold a conversation.**
