## 2. What this thread built (all live and tested unless noted)

**Delivery (Uber Direct)**
- Test keys fixed. The full flow works in the sandbox: a robo courier delivers to "dropped off", with webhooks.
- OrderFare texts the customer at **picked up / delivered / canceled**. Uber's own SMS alerts are turned off in the Uber dashboard.
- Uber is the default for new shops. Who delivers is set by OrderFare at setup and can't be changed in shop chat; owners see it read-only, and staff get a confirmation dialog.
- Each order books the courier it was quoted with, and bookings are "leave at door" (also when nobody answers).
- Test shops (`is_test`, i.e. Vito's) are always test mode: test Stripe, Uber sandbox, no "test mode" keyword, 24/7 hours, `prep_minutes` 2 so the sandbox courier arrives in about 5 minutes.

**Money and payments**
- Checkout now charges on the **shop's Stripe account** (Standard or Express, decided in docs/BUSINESS.md, never re-ask).
- The application fee is `$1.49 + (Uber orders) delivery + tip − 2.9% of that` (`_shared/fees.ts`).
- A live-money charge can never fall back to OrderFare's account. Before this, every order was charged on the platform account with no split.
- The fee is defined once in `_shared/fees.ts` ($1.49). The grader, test checks and welcome email no longer assume $0.99.
- **Refunds** (`refund-order` + `_shared/refund-rules.ts`):
  - only the shop refunds, through OrderFare: the Expo screen "Cancel / refund" or shop chat "refund order N"
  - preview first, then cancel the courier, then split the refund
  - shop-caused Uber fees go on `shops.balance_owed_cents` and are recovered from the next orders
  - the customer gets a refund text with the reason
  - refunds made directly in Stripe raise an issue
  - the function now requires the owner, an admin, or the internal secret; before, it accepted anyone
- "cancel my order" after paying points the customer to the shop (with delivery-fee wording once a driver is en route). A bare CANCEL still unsubscribes (carrier rule) but adds how to cancel.
- Financial Reporting fixed: the stale deploy, owners getting 403, admins scoped to a home tenant. Test shops show test orders.

**Ordering engine**
- Options offered once ("Cheesesteak: … Want it like that, or any toppings?").
- The usual meaning of a word is confirmed (`menu_items.meta.primary_for`): "cheesesteak" → "you mean the Cheesesteak Sandwich? We also have …". "No" lists all options.
- An included side is asked about (`meta.includes`): "the fries that come with the chicken fingers, or an additional order?". EXTRA/NO.

## 3. Decisions made this thread (don't re-ask)

- Uber is the default; shop delivery is a deliberate setup choice made by OrderFare.
- The shop is the seller. OrderFare only owns charging the confirmed total, a matching ticket and booking the courier, and credits the shop for its own errors.
- Refund rules as in `_shared/refund-rules.ts` and terms §5.
- Card fees: the shop pays the flat 30¢ and the % on its food and tax; OrderFare pays the % on its own share.
- Late delivery is an Uber claim and the food stays paid.
- "Leave at door".
- Vito's menu mirrors Jack's. Unknown options are an onboarding item: the owner walkthrough recorded in Granola answers the compiler's owner questions (backlog).
- Side-upgrade prices are left off until an owner gives them.
- Erin is full admin.
