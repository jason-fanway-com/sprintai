The problem

Aggregators take 30% and own your customer.

That's not a service fee. That's a tax on every plate you serve — and when the app owns the customer, you're renting access to people who already love your food.

📉

The old way — delivery apps

15–30% commission on every order. On $40,000 in monthly orders, that's $6,000–$12,000 gone. The customer belongs to the app, not you. You can't text your own regulars. You can't tell them the special. And when the app raises its rates, your margins just get thinner.

📱

The Sprint way — your number, your customers

Flat $99/month + $0.99 per order. Same $40,000 in orders costs you $495. You keep $39,505 instead of $28,000–$34,000. The customer list is yours. The number is yours. You text them when the white truffle special is back. No middleman. No commission. Just your shop and your regulars.

How it works

Three steps. Two weeks. Zero tech skills.

1

We give you a dedicated text-line number.

Your customers text a local number — the same one on your receipts, your window, and your Instagram bio. No app to download, no account to create.

2

Your customers text it like they'd text anyone.

"Large pepperoni and a dozen wings." The system understands it. Confirms the order. Sends a payment link. Remembers "the usual" for next time.

3

You get a ticket. They pick up.

Orders arrive as structured tickets. Payment is handled. Your staff makes the food. The customer walks in, grabs it, done. You keep 100% of the margin and 100% of the relationship.
